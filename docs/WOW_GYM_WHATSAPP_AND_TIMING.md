## Notes on the message

- **Leads with a compliment, then a specific, verifiable observation** (Ahead Fitness's app) — not a generic "I can help your business grow."
- **Names the loss in his language** — "expired members walking in free" and "losing members to gyms that are easier to find." Both are things a gym owner feels viscerally.
- **Removes risk** — 2 hours, first month free, no obligation.
- **Asks for a small yes** — 10 minutes, not a "partnership."
- **No links in the first message.** Links from unknown numbers get flagged and can trigger WhatsApp's spam filter on a business number. Send the demo link *after* he replies.
- **Keep it in one message.** Splitting into multiple texts reads as a bot.
